Before outage
GNSS initializes position and heading, while pre-outage straight segments provide the gyro-bias estimate.
During outage
GNSS measurements are masked. Speed and yaw are estimated from inertial data and fused with the dead-reckoning state.
Reacquisition
GNSS becomes available again and the navigation state can transition back to GNSS-aided operation.
